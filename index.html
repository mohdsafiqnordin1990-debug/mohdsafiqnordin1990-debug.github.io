Product Video Poster

A web tool that turns a product picture into a short vertical video with an AI-written script and voiceover, then posts it to the owner's TikTok account.

It uses TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API. The video is generated from the picture you upload, and nothing is posted without your authorization.
