Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

What this app does

Product Video Poster creates a short video from a product picture you upload and, with your permission, publishes it to your TikTok account.

Information we collect

  • TikTok authorization: when you log in with TikTok, we receive an access token and your TikTok open ID, plus basic profile information (such as display name) allowed by the permissions you approve.
  • Uploaded content: the product pictures you upload and the videos, scripts and captions generated from them.

How we use it

We use this information only to generate videos and to post them to your TikTok account when you ask us to. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Third-party services

Your uploaded picture may be sent to an AI service (Google Gemini) to write the script, and a text-to-speech service to create the voiceover. TikTok receives the finished video when you publish it.

Storage and deletion

Generated files are stored only as long as needed to create and publish your video. You can revoke this app's access at any time in your TikTok settings under Security and permissions. To request deletion of your data, contact us at the email below.
